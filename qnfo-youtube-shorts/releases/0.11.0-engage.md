# qnfo-youtube-shorts 0.11.0-engage (2026-11-09)

Pillar reach. YT-ENGAGE-1, from YouTube Studio's own feedback: 30 uploads, 6 likes, 0 comments; 20-25% of viewers stayed past the first seconds on the weak Shorts; the 27 s Short held 64% against 18-40% for 50-65 s ones; the ending cited a paper and sent viewers away.

- Length: script 60-90 words (was 85-110), 25-40 s target; `scriptIssues` fails over 95 words, a first sentence over 12 words, jargon in the first 30 words.
- Hook: spoken paradox in the first 1.5 s, shown big in beat 1 (sound-off viewers).
- Teaching order: everyday comparison before the academic term, one counterintuitive idea.
- Ending: the director writes a separate `question` (specific, debate-worthy); the closing beat speaks and shows it ("Tell us in the comments"), the paper moves to the description (which now opens with the question). Old citation closing remains only as fallback.
- First comment: the question is posted as the channel's top-level comment after upload (needs `youtube.force-ssl`, added to the consent scopes; a refusal logs `youtube.comment_scope`). The API cannot pin: pinning stays a Studio click.
- Metrics: hourly read adds `avg_view_pct`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares`, `subs_gained` to `youtube_videos`.
