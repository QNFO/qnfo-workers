# qnfo-email 2.16.0-codeagent (2026-11-26)

NOTICE-REPLY-NEVER-VERBATIM-1: a reply to a [handoff] notice is feedback to the system. Typed text is never mailed to the sender; only a bare SEND sends the exact draft the notice showed. "I haven't corresponded with this person" blocks the sender and its domain, suppresses the contact, and files NOTICE-MISCLASSIFIED-1. The cold-sender notice no longer claims a past correspondence, and bulk relay senders (rsend.*, mail-provider relays, throwaway TLDs) never raise a notice.
