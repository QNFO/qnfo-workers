## 1. Who we are (one paragraph)

QNFO is a two-year-old, solo-run, AI-assisted research platform that has produced an open, DOI-registered corpus of approximately 1,000 method papers across seven program areas: ultrametric physics, laws of form, information physics, paradigm engineering, consilience, platform engineering, and demos. The platform runs on open infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers/D1/R2/Vectorize) with a documented audit discipline — citation audits, post-publication adversarial review, and ignorance auditing — and a prior IPFS/decentralized deployment history. QWAV is the companion program for quantum computing theory, including the JPCUB energy-benchmarking initiative.

## 2. The problem we solve

Two converging gaps:

1. **AI-assisted research lacks auditability.** High-volume AI-assisted knowledge production is untrustworthy without systematic verification. QNFO's published methodology (Universal Ignorance Audit, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984; pipeline lessons, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901983) makes not-knowing and AI involvement legible, auditable, and reproducible — turning "AI slop" risk into a quality signal.
2. **Quantum computing has no energy standard.** Quantum computers have a proven energy advantage on specific problems (arXiv:2305.11212; PRX Quantum 4, 040319), yet no neutral standard measures "energy per correct answer." QWAV's JPCUB (Joules-per-Solution) metric — total system energy per correct solution, platform-agnostic across quantum/GPU/CPU — fills that vacuum, following the SPEC/PUE/MLPerf precedent: the entity that defines the standard first owns it (QWAV Strategy v2.4.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21905166; protocol P0 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21637028; competitive landscape DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21821767; qudit advantage DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21880104).
