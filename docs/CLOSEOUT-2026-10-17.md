## 2. The control-plane outage (found and fixed during closeout)

From ~15:00Z every `INSERT INTO fleet_tick` failed with `D1_ERROR: triggers nested too deep`. The
10-minute tick was dead: no research backpressure, no deploy-drift scan, no remediation probes, no
verification rows. `qnfo-fleet-control` logged nothing between 14:50:36Z and 15:20:36Z.

Root cause: `trg_metric_write_audit` fired on `UPDATE OF last_value` in `metric_registry` and did
`INSERT INTO cloud_ops_events`; the events table carries triggers that update `metric_registry` again,
closing the loop. `fleet_tick` has 43 triggers and most of them write metrics, so every tick walked into it.

Fix (applied): the audit now writes to `metric_write_audit_log`, a table with no triggers.
`ops_config.metric_write_audit_route = own-table` records the reasoning.
Verified: `fleet_tick` inserts again, events resumed at 15:20:36Z, and the audit fires (`referral_30d
621 → 620` recorded and the metric restored).

## 6. Process contracts (owner directive: every step and process has an SLA and multiple paths to done)

Implemented 2026-10-17 as an enforced registry, not a convention:

- `process_contracts` — one row per process: `owner`, `sla_min`, `definition_of_done`, and `paths_json`
  (an ordered list: primary, then remediation/workaround routes that do not depend on the same failing
  component). Seeded with 12 processes: research intake, ground, draft, review, publish; remediation probe;
  code task; deploy; social distribution; errata correction; email send; issue close.
- `v_process_contract_gaps` — per-process state of `sla_state`, `dod_state`, `path_state`;
  `v_process_contract_violations` — only the rows that fail any of them. Currently **12 processes, 0
  violations**.
- `process_contract_valid_ins` / `process_contract_valid_upd` — a BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE trigger that aborts
  any row without an owner, an SLA of at most 120 minutes, a definition of done, or at least two paths.
  Verified: a single-path insert is rejected with
  `PROCESS-CONTRACT-1: a process needs an owner, an SLA of at most 120 minutes, a definition of done, and at
  least two ordered paths`.

Deliberately a table and views rather than another `fleet_tick` trigger: the trigger graph is already deep
enough to fail `remediation_verifications` inserts (section 3), and adding a 44th trigger to the tick would
make that worse.

**Limitation, stated plainly:** the registry guarantees that every declared process *has* an SLA and two or
more paths, and blocks new processes from being declared without them. It does not yet prove at runtime
that a given process met its SLA or actually took a fallback path — that measurement needs the verification
write path from section 3, which is still blocked. Coverage is the 12 top-level processes; individual
sub-steps inside a stage (for example each model rung inside a code task) are covered by their parent
process, not by their own row.
