## 3. Security measures (Art. 32), as found

TLS and HSTS on every public host; secrets only as Worker secrets under a lock; one-time codes and session tokens stored as hashes; subscriber sign-up addresses stored as an unsalted SHA-256 for rate limiting (pseudonymous only, see R1); constant-time comparison of tokens; confirm links that need a POST; capped and rate-limited outreach; personal and research data in separate D1 databases that research workers cannot read; the owner's personal plane has a privacy view, an export and "delete everything".

Known gaps (each has an issue or an owner card): no retention sweep (R4, R10); raw IP addresses in ipatent (R10); GA4 cookies without consent (R12); the non-essential `ipatent_ab` cookie (R10); crawler `From` addresses in Workers Logs (R14); Article 50 disclosure for replies sent in the owner's name (owner card bp26-email-agent-ai-disclosure).
