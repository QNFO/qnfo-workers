`id, slug, form, title, subtitle, lede, body_md, anchor_json, quality_json, word_count, day,
created_at` — **no author, no persona, no byline column**. Nothing in the schema defines a
speaker, so the first person in the prose ("the claim I want to argue", "My inference — and it is
an inference, not something the sources state", "I should not have leaned on it alone") is
generated per piece by the composing model. For this piece that model is **`deepseek-chat`**
(§1). The "I" is a generic essayist stance the model adopts because the form is an essay; it is
not the reader, not the fleet, and not a persistent character. Nothing in the corpus is written

**addressee**.
