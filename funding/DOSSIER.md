## 0. Core pitch (v2, 2026-08-16) — rapid research iteration

Research is not bottlenecked by intelligence; it is bottlenecked by process. Traditional research cycles — question → literature → analysis → verification → publication — take months or years, and most of that time is logistics, not thinking. LLMs can compress the cycle by orders of magnitude **if** paired with protocols, processes, and guardrails designed for research rather than for chatbots. QNFO is a two-year, live demonstration of that thesis: one researcher, ~1,000 open method papers (August 2026 count; dated), every artifact passing through structured audits (citation audits, paper-claim audits, post-publication adversarial review, Universal Ignorance Audit).

**The knowledge graph is the key asset.** It is API-accessible and combines conventional research artifacts with LLM-generated insights — effectively RAG + structured database + graph in one queryable layer that any LLM (agentic or otherwise) can consume. Anyone could tap into the corpus today, or run the initial full research pipeline themselves. None of the technologies are new; what is new is the combination: leveraging existing cloud infrastructure so that any researcher or any AI system can access verified, structured, cross-linked knowledge.

QNFO is open, public-interest research with a funding target: funded by grants first and by paid assessments second, with paid products only once there is an audience (STRATEGY section 8). The work stays open: better insights and better knowledge as public infrastructure. (Changed 2026-10-01, STRATEGY-1; v2 said "explicitly **not** a business ... not monetized".)

## 2. The problem we solve

Two converging gaps:

1. **AI-assisted research lacks auditability.** High-volume AI-assisted knowledge production is untrustworthy without systematic verification. QNFO's published methodology (Universal Ignorance Audit, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901984; pipeline lessons, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21901983) makes not-knowing and AI involvement legible, auditable, and reproducible — turning "AI slop" risk into a quality signal.
2. **Research velocity is bottlenecked by process, not intelligence.** Traditional cycles take too long; LLMs with appropriate protocols/processes/guardrails compress them dramatically. The QNFO knowledge graph (API-accessible, RAG + database + graph combined) is the structured layer that lets any LLM — or any researcher — tap into the corpus or the initial full research pipeline.
