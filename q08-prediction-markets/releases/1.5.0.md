PM-CHARTER-1: price-following is gone. The estimate is a three-model ensemble (three families, none sees the price) over Hacker News and GDELT evidence; a trade needs every member on the same side of the price, a gap of the minimum (10 points by default) and a spread under 25 points. Every resolved estimate is scored against the market price, a 50/50 blend and a control that backs the market favourite (bootstrap intervals), shadow ROI is tracked, and GET /api/charter serves the charter, the evidence and the graduation gate, which only reports. The review loop pauses entries on a 15% drawdown or a significantly worse model (24 h expiry), tunes the minimum gap between 8 and 20 points, logs each change in pt_decision, and publishes eight pm_* metrics and a heartbeat to qnfo-audit. No live executor exists.
