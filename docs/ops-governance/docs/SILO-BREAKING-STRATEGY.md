## 1. The problem, formalized (the Tao observation)

1. A filter bubble is a hub-and-spoke attention network. Hub nodes (Tao-class figures, or the
   arXiv daily feed itself) allocate attention on behalf of thousands; members outsource
   discovery to hubs and shared feeds.
   WHY: information is abundant; attention is the scarce good, and attention is routed through trust.
2. "Un-original novelty" is a network effect, not an idea effect. The same result reads as
   novelty when it enters through an insider (novelty is transferred with trust) and as noise
   when it arrives from an unknown node. Mathematicians cling to every Tao tweet because Tao is
   a pre-filter: his endorsement means the novelty check has already been done by someone they trust.
   WHY: gatekeeping-by-hub is rational under attention scarcity - not malice, and not avoidable
   by louder signaling.
3. Corollaries:
   - Novelty and credibility are transferred, not broadcast. You cannot out-shout a hub; you can
     appear in the hub's incoming stream (citation alerts, the feeds they read).
   - One message, many framings: for each receiving domain, the work must read as an extension of
     that domain's own canon (insider-adjacent), not as an outsider's invention.
   - Sub-hubs are the highest-leverage contacts: mid-tier translators with real followings who
     read across domains. One sub-hub citation or repost crosses the wall for thousands at the
     contact cost of reaching one person.

## 4. New programs (P-G..P-K)

Each program is specified Lamport-style: numbered steps, each carrying WHAT + WHY + SCOPE.

### P-G. arXiv entry via endorsement acquisition

1. G.1 WHAT: deposit flagship records on arXiv (the single largest bubble-crossing feed).
   WHY: closes S2-ZENODO-GAP-1 (Semantic Scholar does not index Zenodo), enters the daily
   reading stream of every target domain, and makes QNFO work citable by arXiv-native papers.
   SCOPE: flagships only (JPC.003 / UMP.012 / UMP.014 + the JPCUB benchmark); not a mirror of
   every version - one arXiv record per paper, updated on major versions only.
2. G.2 WHAT: build the endorsement map: for each flagship, list candidate categories
   (quant-ph; cs.ET / cs.LG; math-ph; cond-mat.stat-mech) and, per category, the authors of the
   arXiv papers the submission cites who are listed as endorsers (the abstract page's "Which
   authors of this paper are endorsers?" link).
   WHY: the endorsement policy (read live 2026-09-04) documents exactly this path; at least one
   positive endorsement is required per category.
   SCOPE: planning rows only - submissions kind=arxiv_endorsement, status=planned.
3. G.3 WHAT: request endorsement only from authors with whom a genuine exchange already exists
   (a citation-thanks reply from P-E, or an RFC reply); one request per contact, never cold,
   never batched.
   WHY: the policy explicitly marks mass or bulk endorsement requests as inappropriate; the
   reputation risk of a cold endorsement ask exceeds its value.
   SCOPE: after 2026-09-15 activation; rate 1 flagship/month.
4. G.4 WHAT: submit and cross-list via the arXiv submission system; log every step in
   submissions (submitted_at + evidence).
   WHY: idempotent, auditable, honest.
   SCOPE: prerequisite = arXiv account + ORCID link (one-time human action HA-2, section 5).

### P-H. Proactive participation (outbound reply-engagement + open reviews)

1. H.1 WHAT: extend the existing multi-venue radar (RELATED-SITES-SURVEY.md / qnfo-venue-radar)
   with a keyword monitor over Bluesky/Mastodon search, X search, HN Algolia, and the LW/AF API:
   "joules-per-compute", "PaQit", "Landauer", "QEC energy", "energy cost of computation",
   "codecarbon", "energy benchmark", "margolus-levitin".
   WHY: threads on these topics are where QNFO results are genuinely relevant; the radar turns
   ambient conversation into participation opportunities.
   SCOPE: read-only monitoring; no auto-posting.
2. H.2 WHAT: triage rule: reply only where a QNFO verified result genuinely answers or advances
   the thread; skip anything self-promotion-adjacent.
   WHY: P-H is the anti-Tao move done right - hubs earn their position by adding value inside
   the conversation; the participation door opens only with substance.
   SCOPE: one triage rule, enforced in the reply path.
3. H.3 WHAT: reply shape: one substantive sentence (a number with its DOI, a pointer to the
   reproduction script, or the relevant paper) + disclosure ("open research group, CC BY on
   Zenodo") + at most one link; maximum one reply per thread; every outbound reply logged to
   qnfo-audit.
   WHY: honest participation, no link-dumping, no spam perception.
   SCOPE: cap 2/day global; Bluesky/Mastodon automatable (GREEN); X replies monitor-only until
   Buffer reply support is verified (AMBER); HN/LW remain human-gated per the survey verdicts.
4. H.4 WHAT: open reviews: 1-2 PREreview/Qeios reviews per month of adjacent preprints (energy
   accounting in ML/QC papers), citing QNFO work only where the science requires it.
   WHY: reviewers are visible inside the bubble; reviews earn standing and reciprocal attention.
   SCOPE: AMBER per the per-platform TOS checks already flagged in the catalog.

### P-I. Hub-ranked contact mining (miner v0.3 spec)

1. I.1 WHAT: extend contacts with a network_position score + hub flag: OpenAlex author
   cited_by counts, GitHub followers/stars, Bluesky/X follower counts, events-radar speaker
   prominence.
   WHY: the sub-hub door (D4) - the top decile of network positions converts at a higher rate
   than the long tail at identical send cost.
   SCOPE: additive schema columns; no new sends, better targeting under the same caps.
2. I.2 WHAT: template routing: top-decile hubs receive the interview template with a
   personalized first sentence drawn from the P-J framing pack; the long tail receives the
   standard RFC.
   WHY: personalization is the difference between a cold email and a conversation for busy
   people - the sub-hub-scale version of the Tao effect.
   SCOPE: after 2026-09-15; same no-repeat bridge and caps.
3. I.3 WHAT: log hub-tier sends distinctly in funnel_daily (kind suffix) so reply-rate-by-tier
   is measurable; register candidate experiment EXP-2026-005 (hub-personalized vs standard,
   aggregate-over-N, same window).
   WHY: honest measurement of whether network position actually converts better.

### P-J. Domain-native framing kits

1. J.1 WHAT: for each of the four target domains - (a) energy-efficient computing / HPC,
   (b) quantum information, (c) ML systems, (d) thermodynamics of computation - build a pack:
   5-10 canonical references (what insiders cite), a vocabulary map (their terms <-> QNFO
   terms), and 2-3 named open problems the QNFO result touches.
   WHY: door D5 - the same result must read as an extension of the receiving bubble's canon;
   "extends the Margolus-Levitin bound..." lands differently from "we built a metric".
   SCOPE: content artifacts qnfo-ops/docs/framing/<domain>.md + a D1 framing_packs table so
   templates render per-domain openings.
2. J.2 WHAT: wire the packs into the existing templates (RFC/interview/pitch/social D7 copy):
   the opening sentence is chosen from the receiving domain's pack, not a generic hook.
   WHY: this is the operational form of CROSSWALK-TRANSLATION-1 and TERMINOLOGY-SILO-LESSONS-1
   applied to outbound messaging.
   SCOPE: template parametrization only; no new send types.
3. J.3 WHAT: partitionality discipline: each pack must connect to >=2 other domains' packs
   (bridge share reported), per the standing bridge-registration doctrine.
   WHY: packs that only echo one bubble rebuild the silo.

### P-K. Bridge-note publication program

1. K.1 WHAT: publish 2-4 short technical notes per year that build GENUINELY on external
   hub-adjacent work - a verified reproduction, extension, or counter-example
   (COMPUTATIONAL-VERIFICATION-1 applies; artifacts deposited).
   WHY: door D2 - the reference graph is the only channel that fires the cited authors' own
   alert streams; a genuine build-on is the strongest possible attention request because it
   asks nothing.
   SCOPE: reuses the existing publish pipeline; Zenodo canonical; CC BY.
2. K.2 WHAT: reference-list discipline: each note cites >=2 works from >=2 different domains
   plus the QNFO corpus node it extends; the citations are the crossing edges.
   WHY: a note that cites only its own silo is an announcement, not a bridge.
   SCOPE: enforced at the publish gate (REFERENCE-TITLE-FIDELITY-1 family).
3. K.3 WHAT: every bridge note feeds P-E citation-thanks and P-G's endorsement map: the cited
   authors are simultaneously the alert receivers and the endorsement candidates.
   WHY: one publication, three inbound channels.
   SCOPE: first candidate topics = the open JPCUB questions (Margolus-Levitin per-operation
   bound; surface-code energy floor) - both adjacent to named, citable external work.
