# qnfo-fleet-dashboard 1.94.0 (2026-11-05)

RM-CMD-FAILURES-1 (pillar autonomy): the failing stage of the /cmd command line is named. GET /api/cmd/failures counts failed and fallback cmd_log rows by stage (hand-off, ops-exec, stream, model, worker-stopped, other) with no prompt text. Root causes fixed: a CodeSession stream turn (kind stream) rendered as an empty card on /cmd and the terminal client printed nothing, and its cmd_log row was swept as abandoned three minutes after it started although qnfo-agents had finished it (2 of 2 owner stream turns). The page now renders the stream (text, tool steps, model, tokens, cost) and resumes the session; the row links to its session and is settled by the sweep, the job poll and the end of the stream; a refused CodeSession or ops-exec hand-off is logged as a stage row. A refused microphone names a site Permissions-Policy refusal (the qnfo.org zone rule sets microphone=() over the worker's own header) instead of blaming the browser.
