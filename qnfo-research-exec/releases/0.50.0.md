# qnfo-research-exec 0.50.0 (2026-11-04)

PAPER-CANONICAL-1 (pillar research, owner directive 2026-11-04): every stored paper body is the new-release shape. A revision, a correction and the hourly paperFormatTick now store the canonical text (no YAML front matter, byline or metadata block, repeated or wrapped title, in-body Version History, Changelog, correction notice or revision banner); rlWithHistory no longer writes a history section (paper_revision_log, the ledger and the Versions tab hold it). The tick refuses, and lists in paper_format_state.refusedList, any body that would lose more than 35 percent of its words, a reference list or unexplained headings, keeps updated_at and the version for format-only changes, and puts the new body in the R2 mirror. Owner-written qnfo-2025 papers keep their own history section. The previous body of every rewrite stays verbatim in paper_change_bodies.

Review fixes before the first deploy: keywords and a valid non-10.5281 DOI that only a header held are moved to the empty papers columns (the ORCID, ISNI, contact and dates stay in the ledger copy and the change_reason says so); a level-2 heading counts as the repeated title only at near-total word overlap and before the first prose block, a level-3 heading becomes the subtitle only straight under the title above a metadata block, level 4 never; a history section is only Version History, Revision History, Revision Notes, Changelog, Change Log or Correction Notice (unnumbered, holding a version or a date) and ends at the first heading that is not history or at the first line that is not a list item, table row, rule, indented line or version note; the tick refuses a paper whose removed history is more than 15 percent of its words; a labelled prose paragraph after the abstract stays; the abstract column is never overwritten by a derived one and a body with no abstract of its own gets the column abstract; a failed mirror write is kept in paper_format_state.mirrorRetry and retried first on the next tick.
