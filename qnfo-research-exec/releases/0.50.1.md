# qnfo-research-exec 0.50.1 (2026-11-04)

PAPER-CANONICAL-1 review fixes (PR 7241 merged before they landed): keywords and a valid DOI that only a header held are moved to the empty columns; stricter repeated-title, subtitle and history rules (a history section ends at the first non-history heading or non-list line; only unnumbered Version History, Revision History, Revision Notes, Changelog, Change Log and Correction Notice that hold a version or a date; refusal when the removed history is over 15 percent of the words); the abstract column is never overwritten by a derived one; labelled prose after the abstract stays; a failed mirror write is retried on the next tick.
