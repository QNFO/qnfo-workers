# PLCY — Map–Territory Realization Gate & Ontic Gate (DPRF G1/G2)

**Authority:** enacted by QNFO.RES.032 (Distinction-Primitive Research Framework) P0, 2026-08-29,
under ADR-2026-007 governance as cited by the WBS docs (ADR full text not located in cloned repos — P0.1 R11; provisional until located) · **Scope:** all QNFO/QWAV research programs (provisional)
**Source record:** QNFO/qnfo-research, branch res/paper/distinction-primitive-research-framework,
FRAMEWORK.md (v0.1)

---

## 1. Policy statement

Every research claim in any QNFO program carries, at registration time:

1. a **framework level** on the pre-arithmetic ladder D0–D8 (or a declared span with a bridge list);
2. an **ontic commitment** — `methodological` (default), `heuristic`, or `ontic`;
3. a **map–territory status** — `map`, `bridge`, or `territory`.

Two hard gates govern upward movement toward physical reality.

## 2. G1 — ONTIC-GATE

Distinction (D0) and everything built on it is a **methodological primitive by default**.
No reification to ontic status is permitted unless the commitment is:

- explicit and per claim,
- justified in prose,
- **pre-registered with the claim** (an ontic commitment cannot be retrofitted after a null
  result),
- accompanied by the full G2 triple (protocol, null model, falsification condition).

An `ontic` commitment makes the claim a physics-level (D8) claim regardless of its ladder level.

## 3. G2 — REALIZATION-GATE (map–territory)

A mathematical isomorphism — however exact — is a **map**, not a **territory**. A claim may pass
from `map` to `territory` only through a declared `bridge` carrying, all pre-specified:

1. **measurement protocol** — observable, system, instrument, resolution;
2. **null model** — what the data would look like if the structure were absent (matched-density
   nulls are the canonical pattern for spectral claims);
3. **falsification condition** — the observation that would refute the claim.

A claim without the triple is demoted: `territory` → `bridge`, `bridge` → `map`. A `map` carries
no empirical force and must not be worded as if it did. Missing only the null model or only the falsifier → demote one rung; dual G1+G2 violations → both demotions apply, lower class wins (P0.1 R9).

## 4. Reporting rules (inherited from RES.030 Ch. 9–11 discipline)

- Effect sizes, not only p-values; the reportable object is the full discriminator curve.
- Unfolding sensitivity: ≥ 3 unfoldings; single-unfolding signatures are not signatures.
- Pre-registered multiple-comparison correction (two-point-derived observables are one channel).
- Object separation: never report a prime-gap result as a zeros result, and conversely.

## 5. Defect classes (audit-registered)

| Defect | Rule violated | Fix |
|:-------|:--------------|:----|
| `LEAK-X-Y` | R3 (level skipping) | declare intermediate bridges or demote level |
| `ONTIC-SMUGGLE` | G1 | declare commitment or rewrite as methodological |
| `MAP-AS-TERRITORY` | G2 | supply triple or demote to `map` |
| `OBJ-CONFLATE` | R5 | separate the objects (primes vs zeros; two-point vs one-point) |

## 6. Where it applies

- **P0** core-claim lock: claim sheet (DPRF template T-1) attached to PROJECT-PLAN.
- **P1** due diligence: corpus hits tagged with the DPRF level legend (T-2).
- **P4** red team: standing adversary positions = the four defect classes.
- **P5** publication gates: GATE-ONTIC-1 and GATE-REALIZATION-1 appended to the existing gate set
  (worked precedents: UMP.014's map-territory clause (red-team round 3) and RES.030's matched-density null discipline).
- **Audit**: the four defect classes join the anti-pattern register.

## 7. Non-goals

This policy regulates **claims about the world and their wording**. It does not restrict formal
mathematics (maps are fine), does not answer whether D0 should ever be ontic (open problem,
FRAMEWORK.md §6.1), and makes no empirical claims of its own.
