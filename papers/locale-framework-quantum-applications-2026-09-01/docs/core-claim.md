# Core Claim — QNFO.UMP.012 (P6 lock)

**Locked:** 2026-08-17 · **Owner:** Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas

## C₀ (the locked claim)

**The locale framework transfers to quantum computing. Every innovation and practical-application claim of quantum computing — "advantage", "utility", "fault tolerance by 2030", "CV outperforms DV" — is a conditional truth with an implicit locale. The seams where those claims fail to transfer are where the practitioner-relevant physics lives; the practitioner (engineer, investor, buyer) experiences a rendering (spec sheets, benchmarks, roadmaps), not the substrate; and the natural observables of the practitioner domain are counts and ratios — of which joules-per-solution is the composite scale primitive.**

## The four moves (mapped from UMP.011)

- **C1 locale-conditionality:** advantage/utility/roadmap claims hold only inside explicit locales (error-rate regimes, problem classes, energy budgets, measurement models).
- **C2 map-territory:** practitioner maps (quantum volume, fidelity, gate counts, roadmap dates) fail at seams; the territory that transfers is energy accounting.
- **C3 interface underdetermination (falsifiable core):** the substrate metric of the practitioner interface is not decidable from inside that interface; what cannot be decided can still be probed — a rendering with structure leaks structure (T6: Bruhat-Tits buildings inside Clifford+R exact synthesis).
- **C4 scale primitives:** qubit/T-counts are counts; error rates are ratios; advantage is a ratio of counts that is a function of the energy ratio; joules-per-solution is the composite primitive — externally theorem-backed by T1/T2.

## Falsifiability register (inherited, instantiated)

- F1: a practical-application claim that transfers across ALL seams without degradation (survives bounded-energy translation, classical-simulation attacks, end-to-end energy accounting) falsifies C₀. No such claim exists; QPL 2026 is effectively a falsification search.
- F2: a practitioner-relevant observable that is NOT a count or ratio (falsifies C4's reduction).
- F3: a probe of the practitioner interface that decides the substrate metric (falsifies C3). Open probe-design question — same as UMP.011 F3.
- F4: the seams catalog is retrodictive; new seam-documents that contradict T1–T8's pairing (claim↔document) require catalog revision.

## Premise-depth (what C₀ is NOT)

C₀ does NOT claim: a dynamical theory of seams; that the ultrametric substrate is real (only that it is not decidable from inside, and that structure leaks); that energy is the only currency (only that it is the interconversion currency between paradigms, per external theorems); that the corpus's own rendering is exempt from C3 (Q15 of the UIA seeds this audit).
