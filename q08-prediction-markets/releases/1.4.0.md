Short-term signal strategy (PM-SIGNALS-1): sports markets are excluded; for non-sports markets resolving within 14 days a model reads the last 3 days of GDELT headlines and gives its own probability without seeing the price; a paper trade opens only on a gap of 10 points or more with medium or high confidence, is held to resolution and leaves early only when the market converges to the signal. The older momentum and fade strategies are off by default. Dashboard shows the predictions with their evidence-based reasons.
